The big picture

One platform that sells cars and runs itself

Shoppers get a fast, helpful, Carvana-style experience. The dealership gets a control panel to manage every part of the site — inventory, marketing, leads, chat, content and analytics — without touching code.

AI throughout

⚙️

Self-serve admin

For your customers

The shopping experience

Designed to feel like the big national brands — clean, fast, and helpful — while keeping Carson's honest, family-run voice.

  • Inventory browsing — filter by body type, make, price and monthly payment; "shop by style / budget / payment" tiles on the home page.
  • Vehicle detail pages — photos, specs, AI overview, financing math, and live "X people viewing / just arrived" social proof.
  • AI Car Finder — shoppers describe what they want in plain English and get matched instantly.
  • CarFinder alerts — can't find it? Customers save a request and get auto-notified (text/email) the moment a match arrives.
  • Watch this car — price-drop alerts on a specific vehicle, capturing fence-sitters.
  • Financing tools — payment calculator, an AI financing explainer, and trade-in valuation.
  • Live + AI chat — real salesperson during business hours, AI after hours — and AI auto-covers if the team is slow to reply.
  • Service department — dedicated pages for service, tires, specials and parts with booking forms.
  • Meet the team, guides, reviews & Instagram — trust-building content that's all editable in admin.

Under the hood

Technology & integrations

Next.js 14 Supabase (database) OpenAI (AI features) kie.ai · GPT Image 2 (banner art) Google Sheets (inventory sync) Web analytics Webhook → Twilio (SMS alerts) Netlify hosting Instagram (Behold)
A few items need accounts/keys to go fully live — e.g. the SMS/Twilio automation, the banner-generation key, and email delivery. These are configuration steps, not new development, and can be switched on whenever the client is ready.
