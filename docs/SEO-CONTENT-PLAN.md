## Not content, but the biggest local lever: Google Business Profile
The map pack for "used cars halifax" is O'Regan's Green Light (367 reviews),
O'Regan's Wholesale (191) and Auction Direct (583). Carson Exports isn't in the
top 12. A steady flow of new Google reviews, complete GBP categories, photos and
posts matters more for "used cars near me / dartmouth" than any page.

## Skip
Competitor brand names (O'Regan's, Steele, Princess Auto, Carlsons, Imports
Motors, Clutch) and unrelated businesses (salvage, motorcycles, RVs).
